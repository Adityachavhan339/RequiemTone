# RequiemTone
RequiemTone is a small electronic instrument that lets you play notes, make little melodies, and create retro video-game sounds.
The idea is to create a simple electronic instrument with eight buttons, two knobs, and a piezo sounder.

The Parts I Am Using:

    -8 physical buttons for playing notes or triggering sounds
    -2 potentiometers for adjusting sound parameters
    -Piezo sound output
    -USB-powered Raspberry Pi Pico
    -Custom-designed PCB
    -Planned custom firmware for generating sounds and melodies


What Its Gonna Do:
The Raspberry Pi Pico reads all buttons as digital inputs and the two potentiometers as analog inputs.
The firmware will use these inputs to generate sound through the piezo sounder.
The buttons will be programmed to play different notes, while the potentiometers can control parameters such as tempo and pitch.
